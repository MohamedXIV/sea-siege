SEA SIEGE — Tactical Field Manual

1. Floating Platforms & Territory

Expand outward from your 3×3 Command Core by placing Floating Platforms (25 Salvage) orthogonally adjacent to existing connected platforms.

Superstructures (Shipyard, Workshop, Salvage Extractor, Defensive Cannon) must be placed on top of completed, connected platforms. If an enemy destroys a bridge platform, isolated buildings go OFFLINE!

2. Salvage Economy

Salvage is the lifeblood of your industrial outpost. Gather it in three ways:

  • Build Salvage Extractors near golden salvage deposits.
  • Select Scout Hydrofoils or Gunboats and Right-Click a Salvage Deposit to dredge scrap directly.
  • Destroy enemy warships and structures for combat salvage bounties.
3. Fleet Production & Naval Combat

Construct a 2×2 Naval Shipyard with at least one adjacent open water cell. Queue Scout Hydrofoils (fast recon), Ironclad Gunboats (brawlers), and Dreadnought Siege Monitors (long-range base breakers).

Ship turrets rotate independently of the hull. Build an Industrial Workshop to passively repair nearby ships/structures and research Fleet Weapon Upgrades.

4. Controls & Victory Conditions
  • Left-Click / Drag: Select ship(s) or inspect structure.
  • Right-Click Water: Move selected fleet in formation.
  • Right-Click Enemy: Focus-fire target with turrets.
  • Victory: Destroy all rival Command Cores or lead the Sudden Death Tiebreaker score.
